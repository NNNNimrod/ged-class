Remember: Write a number in scientific notation as a value from 1 up to, but not including, 10, times a power of 10. For a number greater than or equal to 10, the exponent is positive. For a number between 0 and 1, the exponent is negative. To compare two numbers that are already in scientific notation, look first at the powers of 10. The number whose power of 10 is larger is the greater number.

Part C. Short answers.

  1. A delivery drone's longest trip is \(12{,}500\) meters. Write that distance, in meters, in scientific notation.
  2. A smoke-detector sensor can react in \(3.5 \times 10^{-3}\) second. Write that time, in seconds, in standard notation.
  3. A ferry route is \(4.85 \times 10^{5}\) meters long. Write that length, in meters, in standard notation.
  4. A factory produced 45 million bolts last year. Write 45 million in scientific notation.
  5. Which number is greater, \(7.1 \times 10^{6}\) or \(9.4 \times 10^{5}\)? Write the greater number in scientific notation.
